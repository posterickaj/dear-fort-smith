title: ArDot doesn't need any more cookies.
description: >-
  Adding lanes can relieve traffic for a little while.

  But everyone who was holding off on driving before the widening (have you ever thought, "I don't feel like dealing with traffic right now?") is given a lower price to travel when a highway is expanded, and they choose to travel more often. And soon traffic returns to right where it was before you paid for that extra lane.

  Doesn't mean all highway expansions are bad. Just that engineers have a bad track record of predicting how their projects will actually affect traffic, even when they account for the traffic they create. Highways should be expanded to accommodate economic growth, not to accommodate personal comfort.
