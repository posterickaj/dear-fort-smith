A parking space may be empty most of the day, but the city still has to service the land around it. Streets still have to reach it. Drainage still has to handle the runoff. Water and sewer lines still have to stretch past it. Police, fire, EMS, trash, and maintenance crews still have to cover the longer distances created by a parking-heavy development pattern.\
\
That is the part people miss. The asphalt may be private, but the infrastructure pattern is public.\
\
My non-sophisticated math came out to just over $1 trillion in public infrastructure costs related to empty parking in The United States.
