---
title: The rules of supply and demand get real wonky when the thing cannot be
  created or destroyed.
description: >-
  Supply & Demand is real and it works. As demand for something increases,
  businesses can make more of it to gain some share of that market, keeping
  prices low.


  But you can't create more land. There is no way to compete with Ceasar's Palace once the land all around it has been built on. YIMBYs have to confront the reality that more housing in desirable places is a win, but will not solve the long-term problem of elevated land rents. Eventually you need to find a way to let everyone share in the location value they collectively create.
