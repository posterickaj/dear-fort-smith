title: Without Land Value Tax, land values are socialized.
description: >-
  [draft text written by human and revised by ChatGPT for clarity and brevity]


  Your house may be private property, but its value is not created privately.


  Your neighbor can raise or lower your property value without your consent. If they renovate or landscape, your property value may rise. If they neglect their property, your value may fall.


  That is an involuntary redistribution of wealth.


  HOAs try to manage this problem by restricting property rights. They say: because your choices affect everyone else, everyone else gets a say in your choices.


  That is wrong.


  A better system would respect property rights while recognizing that land value is socially created. If your land value rises because the neighborhood made your location more valuable, some of that unearned gain should be returned to the people who helped create it. If your actions impose real costs on your neighbors, you should be responsible for those costs.


  In other words: stop trying to preserve land values through private neighborhood governments and aesthetic control.


  Tax the land value.


  Only then can your home truly be yours.
